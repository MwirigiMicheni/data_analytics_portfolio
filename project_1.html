PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding customer purchasing behaviour

This project analysed 3,900 purchase records to explore customer spending patterns, product preferences, and subscription behaviour.

Using Python, SQL, and Power BI, I prepared and analysed the data, investigated business questions, and developed a dashboard to communicate the findings.

The project deliverables include a technical analysis, a stakeholder-focused report, and an interactive dashboard for exploring customer purchasing patterns. View dashboard ↗
